Trading up: "Hi, Claude!"

January 2026

Subscriptions. So. Many. Subscriptions. Which brings me to my first new one of the year: I'm getting cozy with Claude (and cancelling ChatGPT).

I think Claude and Gemini will make the perfect duo: responsible, polite, acutely funny, and multi-modal. (I'm loving the graphics from Nano Banana!) I could use a thought partner for this site. A couple, to be precise.

What draws me to Claude

  • Anthropic has shown again and again that it cares about humans.
  • Dario Amodei, the CEO, is thoughtful. I don't agree with everything he writes, but he puts bold predictions and warnings in writing, which means he's willing to be publicly wrong.
  • Engineers I know at Google speak well of Claude, and they use it alongside Gemini.
  • OpenAI continues to feel ultra-competitive, which gives me the ick.
  • LLMs aren't just "business" to me. They have the power to transform so much of what we do, and I'd rather most of that stay in the lab until we know more. I guess we're all scientists now.

It's a new year, I'm starting to publish, and I'm getting my team in place. I'm excited to make it a crew of two.

Let's take it for a spin

I loaded it up and subscribed. Right away I landed in Billing, where I could just as easily cancel. No dark patterns, no maze. The settings menu is clear and easy to follow, so I took the tour.

  • General: I set my preferences: Germanic roots over Latin, preferred; humor over niceties. And then, wow: a dyslexia-friendly font. This is awesome.
  • Account: deleting your account is easy to find. This is like a dream. (Ahem, ChatGPT, where do I delete my account?)
  • Privacy: exporting my data is easy. Noted: if I ever leave, I'll do this first. And if I come back, I can bring it with me instead of repeating myself.
  • Capabilities: "Manage memory" brings me here. I get to choose whether Claude remembers past chats, which will be great for carrying context. There's also a section for skills: custom instructions, like "our philosophy" or a brand style guide, that Claude applies to my work.1
  • Connectors: this is where I can link GitHub and other accounts. I'll keep it limited for now, but pulling files straight from Google Drive could be really useful. I can also add a remote MCP server, which I'd like to figure out this year. Stay tuned for that post.
  • Claude Code: cool, it's like Gemini CLI. The perfect coding buddy. Maybe I'll get to it in 2026, too.

Everything is clean and usable. It's brilliant UX content design: easy to join, easy to leave, easy to read. I'm loving Claude already.

P.S. When I asked Gemini about this partnership, it gave a fantastic answer: "If Claude handles the philosophy and the 'human' side (Anthropic's strong suit), I can handle the heavy lifting, data processing, and integration with your Google ecosystem."
