chasing wind

November 2026

i stare in mindful melodies

shielded by one’s grace.

I wish upon a wandering star

how far my legs can pace.

my will relentless by demands

provisioned by this space.

the moving chairs and squatting bears

a winding town’s embrace

from here or there to anywhere

you never showed your face

until t’was head, the whistler’s bell

you hid from our shared fate

with less of one, that single foil

you’d try to be my mate

in ‘stead a breaking, crashing toll

that led to this debate

shall i become one less than you

til whence we congregate

to tie a knot of lesser two

well, both more than one slate

I hinder “less than me?” i say

is nothing whilst I play

for you I’m me, for me I’m you

together we’re all day.

“Until we meet as lesser two,”

you harkon, I’ll be free

I beg of you, if that be true

it’s time for you and me.

You played too long

you ran too far, the goal has fallen suit

I waited here, and missed your cue,

and now it’s just pursuit.
