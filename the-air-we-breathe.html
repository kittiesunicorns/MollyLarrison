the air we breathe is ancient

November 2026

a whispering spirit

calling new beginnings

with old familiar friends

conversations wander

long past bedtime

distractions from aching limbs

grant alms, Protectors

evoke our curious gifts

inspired by deep connections

wonderment eases

sea-fallen sky lanterns

crafted on an old oak floor

with peeking nails

clobbered by an ancient rug

worn from afternoon prayers
